Strategy Backtests

Compare each strategy with EW universe, not just QQQ: both pick from the same index members, so beating EW is the real test. A strategy that only wins in-sample is probably fitted to the past, and one whose CAGR collapses in the cost table needs cheaper execution than you can get.

Charts are rebased to $1 at the period start; stats are computed for the period.

Strategy definitions

Universe: the Nasdaq-100 as it was on each date since 2007; a stock is sold when it leaves the index. Former members Yahoo no longer prices (mostly acquired or delisted companies) are missing, so some survivorship bias remains. Signals use data through each close and trade at the next bar. Cash earns 0%. For research only, not investment advice.
